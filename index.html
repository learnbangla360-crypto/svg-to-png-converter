How to convert SVG to PNG

To convert SVG to PNG, drop your SVG file into the tool, pick a scale and background, and press Convert & Download. Your browser draws the vector art at the size you chose and saves a PNG to your device. Nothing is uploaded.

  1. Drop one or more SVG files into the box.
  2. Check the previews, and remove any you don't want.
  3. Choose a scale (1x to 4x) and a background.
  4. Press Convert & Download.

This SVG to PNG converter turns scalable vector graphics into regular PNG images without sending your files anywhere. SVG files are made of shapes and paths, so they look sharp at any size, but many apps, social sites and upload forms only accept pixels. Here you choose the output scale, keep a transparent background or fill it with a color, and download a PNG in one click. It works on a single logo or a batch of up to 100 files.

Step-by-step guide to converting SVG to PNG

1. Add your SVG files

Drag SVG files onto the box or tap it to browse. Use Add files to keep adding more. Each preview shows the file name, its size in pixels and its file size. The checkerboard behind a preview shows transparent areas.

2. Choose the scale

At 1x the PNG matches the size written in the SVG. Pick 2x, 3x or 4x for a larger, sharper PNG, for example for high-density screens or print. The longest side is capped at 8,000 pixels.

3. Choose the background

Transparent is best for logos and icons you'll place on other backgrounds. Pick White or Black when a platform doesn't handle transparency well or you want a solid backdrop.

4. Convert and download

Press Convert & Download. Each PNG saves with the original name and a .png extension. With several files, your browser may ask to allow multiple downloads. Choose Allow. Need a different size afterward? Use Resize Image.

Expert tips and common mistakes

  • Export larger than you think you need. A 3x or 4x PNG can be shrunk later, but a small PNG can't be enlarged cleanly.
  • Outline your text. Text in an SVG uses fonts on your device. Converting text to paths in your design app guarantees it looks the same.
  • Embed images before exporting. Linked pictures inside an SVG won't load. Embedded ones will.
  • Keep transparency for logos. A white background looks like a box on a colored page.

Troubleshooting

  • "Skipped" message. The file isn't a valid SVG, is over 20 MB, or the batch is full. Open it in a browser to check it displays, then try again.
  • The PNG is blank. The SVG may have no visible shapes, or the artwork sits outside its viewBox. Check the file in a browser or design app.
  • The font looks wrong. The SVG uses a font that isn't installed on your device. Convert text to outlines in your design app and export again.
  • Images are missing. They're linked, not embedded. Embed them in the SVG, then convert.
  • The PNG is tiny. The SVG declares a small size. Pick 2x, 3x or 4x in Scale.
  • Conversion fails at 4x. The result would exceed 8,000 pixels or your device's canvas memory. Use a lower scale.
  • Only some files downloaded. Your browser blocked multiple downloads. Allow downloads for this site and convert again.

Glossary

  • SVG: Scalable Vector Graphics, an image format written in XML that describes shapes and paths.
  • Vector: artwork defined by math, so it stays sharp at any size.
  • Raster: artwork made of pixels, like PNG, with a fixed resolution.
  • viewBox: the SVG attribute that defines its coordinate area and proportions.
  • Alpha channel: the layer that stores how transparent each pixel is.

Frequently asked questions

Is this SVG to PNG converter free?

Yes. You can convert as many SVG files as you like at no cost. There's no watermark, no trial and no paid tier. Drop in your files, press the button and the PNG files download straight to your device.

Are my SVG files uploaded anywhere?

No. Your files are processed in your browser and are never uploaded. The page reads each SVG on your device, draws it on a canvas and saves a PNG locally. Closing the tab clears everything from memory.

Do I need to sign up or install anything?

No. There's no account, no email and no app. Open the page in a modern browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, drop in your SVG files and convert. It works on desktop, tablets and phones.

How do I convert SVG to PNG with a transparent background?

Leave the Background menu on Transparent, which is the default. Any area your SVG doesn't paint stays transparent in the PNG. Choose White or Black only when you want a solid background behind the artwork.

How do I get a high-resolution PNG from an SVG?

Pick 2x, 3x or 4x in the Scale menu. SVG is vector art, so it's redrawn at the larger size instead of stretched, and edges stay sharp. Output is capped at 8,000 pixels on the longest side.

Why does the text in my PNG use a different font?

An SVG drawn as an image can't load web fonts, so text uses fonts installed on your device. For reliable results, convert text to outlines or paths in your design app before exporting the SVG.

Why are linked images missing from the PNG?

For safety, browsers don't load external files referenced inside an SVG that is drawn as an image. Images that are embedded directly in the SVG file as data work fine. Embed linked pictures before converting.

Can I convert many SVG files at once?

Yes. You can add up to 100 SVG files in one go. Each PNG downloads as its own file, one after another. Your browser may ask permission to download multiple files, so choose Allow when it does.

Is there a file size limit?

Each SVG can be up to 20 MB, and the PNG can be up to 8,000 pixels wide or tall. Very large outputs may still fail on older phones because browsers limit canvas memory. Try a lower scale if that happens.

Does it work offline?

Partly. You need to load the page once. After that, the conversion itself needs no connection, so you can keep the tab open, go offline and still convert SVG files that are already on your device.
